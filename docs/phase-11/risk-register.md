## Stop conditions

Stop a live test on an unexpected write outside the disposable page, a duplicate/ambiguous identity, incomplete native QA, an unbounded/repeated model loop, a failed checkpoint before mutation, or a mismatch between the loaded manifest and built artifacts. Preserve the diagnostic and already confirmed work; do not clear storage or reset the canvas to manufacture a pass.
